# Returns and Exchanges

Synthetic policy for the CallVerse store. The 7-day right of withdrawal and the 30/90-day
defect periods follow the Brazilian Consumer Defense Code (CDC, articles 49 and 26); the
procedures are assumptions.

## Right of withdrawal: returning within 7 days
<!-- intents: return_product, return_product_online -->

For online purchases the customer may return any product within 7 days of delivery, for any
reason, with a full refund of the product and the original shipping fee. The return shipping
is free. The product should be returned with its accessories and, where possible, its
original packaging.

## Returns after 7 days
<!-- intents: return_product -->

After the 7-day period, a product can be returned only if it is defective:

- within 30 days of delivery for non-durable products (for example food or cosmetics);
- within 90 days of delivery for durable products (for example electronics, furniture, appliances).

Defective products are first offered a repair or replacement by the seller. If this is not
done within 30 days, the customer may choose a refund instead.

## How to return a product online
<!-- intents: return_product_online, return_product -->

1. Sign in, open "My orders", select the order and choose "Return an item".
2. Select the item and the reason, and add photos if the item is damaged or defective.
3. A prepaid return label is e-mailed within 1 business day.
4. Drop the parcel at any post office within 7 days of receiving the label.
5. Track the return in "My orders". The refund is approved within 3 business days after the seller receives the item.

If the customer cannot print the label, the advisor can send a return authorisation code the
post office accepts instead.

## Exchanging a product for another size, colour or model
<!-- intents: exchange_product -->

- Exchanges are possible within 30 days of delivery for unused products with their original packaging.
- The exchange is for the same product in another size or colour, at the same price and from the same seller.
- Start the exchange in "My orders" with "Exchange an item". The replacement ships when the returned item is scanned at the post office.
- If the replacement is out of stock, the customer receives a full refund instead.
- For a different product or a different seller, the customer returns the item for a refund and places a new order.

## Who pays for return shipping
<!-- intents: return_product, exchange_product, return_product_online -->

| Situation | Return shipping |
|---|---|
| Right of withdrawal (within 7 days) | Free |
| Defective, damaged or wrong item | Free |
| Exchange for size or colour (after 7 days) | Paid by the customer; a paid label is offered in "My orders" |

## Items that cannot be returned
<!-- intents: return_product, exchange_product -->

For hygiene reasons, opened personal-care, intimate apparel and food products cannot be
returned or exchanged unless they are defective. Custom-made products cannot be exchanged.
