# Customer FAQ

Short answers to the most common customer questions. Each answer points to the full policy.

## Where is my order?
<!-- intents: track_order, track_delivery -->

Open "My orders" and select the order to see its status and estimated delivery date. Once it
has shipped you will find a tracking code there and in the "order shipped" e-mail. Full
details: Order Status and Tracking.

## When will my order arrive?
<!-- intents: delivery_time -->

Your estimated delivery date is shown on the order and in the confirmation e-mail. Most
orders arrive before it. If the date has passed, contact us and we will check with the
carrier. Full details: Delivery Times and Delays.

## My order is late or did not arrive. What can I do?
<!-- intents: delivery_issue -->

If the estimated date has passed, contact us. We check the tracking, and if the parcel has
not moved for 5 business days we open an investigation with the carrier. Lost parcels are
re-shipped or refunded. Full details: Delivery Times and Delays.

## How do I cancel my order?
<!-- intents: cancel_order -->

Use "Cancel order" in "My orders" while the order is not yet invoiced. After it ships you
can refuse the delivery or return it within 7 days of receipt. Full details: Cancellations
and Order Changes.

## Can I change my order or my address?
<!-- intents: change_order -->

The address can be changed until the order is invoiced. Products, sizes and quantities cannot
be changed: cancel and order again. Full details: Cancellations and Order Changes.

## How do I get a refund, and when will I receive it?
<!-- intents: request_refund, refund_status -->

Canceled orders are refunded automatically. For returns, the refund is approved within 3
business days after the seller receives the item. Card refunds show on your next 1 or 2
statements; boleto refunds are paid by bank transfer within 10 business days after you give
your bank details. Full details: Refund Policy.

## How do I return or exchange a product?
<!-- intents: return_product, return_product_online, exchange_product -->

Choose "Return an item" or "Exchange an item" in "My orders". You can return anything within
7 days of delivery free of charge, and exchange unused products for another size or colour
within 30 days. Full details: Returns and Exchanges.

## My product arrived damaged, wrong or incomplete.
<!-- intents: damaged_delivery, wrong_item, missing_item, product_issue -->

Report it within 7 days of delivery with photos. We send the right item or refund you, and
return shipping is free. If items seem missing, check first whether your order ships in
several parcels. Full details: Damaged, Wrong, Missing or Defective Items.

## I have a problem with my payment.
<!-- intents: payment_issue -->

If your card was declined you can retry within 24 hours. A pre-authorisation from a
declined or canceled payment disappears within 5 business days. Boletos must be paid within
3 days. Full details: Payments and Payment Problems.

## How do I get my invoice?
<!-- intents: request_invoice -->

Download it from "My orders" once your order is invoiced; a copy is attached to the
"order shipped" e-mail. Full details: Invoices.
